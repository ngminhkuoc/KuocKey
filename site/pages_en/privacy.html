Privacy

What you type never leaves your device. KuocKey makes no network connections on any platform: no update checks, no crash reports, no feedback forms, no analytics.

What you type

KuocKey reads the keys you press to turn them into Vietnamese text, right on your Mac. Your keystrokes never leave it.

Website addresses

To remember the language for each website, KuocKey reads the domain of the open tab in Safari, Chrome, Arc, Edge, Brave and other Chromium browsers. The first time, macOS asks for Automation permission. Firefox is not supported yet. Domains are stored only on your Mac.

Updates and crash reports

KuocKey does not check for updates and does not send crash reports. To get a new version, download it yourself from GitHub.

The KuocKey keyboard on iPhone

The keyboard does everything on your iPhone: turning keys into Vietnamese, switching English/Vietnamese, suggestions and auto-correct. It never goes online and never sends or stores what you type.

"Allow Full Access" is used only for haptic feedback when you type. Without it the keyboard works fully, just without vibration. If you turn on Diagnostics, the keyboard writes how long each tap took to a file on your device, never the text you type; the file only leaves your device if you tap "Send log" yourself.

The KuocKey app collects no data, has no ads and does no tracking.
